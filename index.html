About Me

I'm a second-year Bachelor of Information and Communication Technology undergraduate at the University of Vavuniya , driven by a simple belief: good technology should feel effortless to use. That belief draws me toward UI/UX Design and Frontend Development — two sides of the same problem: how something looks, and how something works.

My curiosity spans Web Technologies, version control with GitHub , and Human-Computer Interaction. I'm interested not just in how to build an interface, but why a particular layout, colour, or flow makes an experience feel natural. Alongside design, I'm building a strong foundation in software development through Java, C++ , and database-driven systems.

Beyond the classroom, I volunteer with tech and community-driven programs — experience that has taught me as much about leadership and collaboration as my coursework has taught me about code.
